# Trio Bee Bakes Delight

Build this on a standard Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + shadcn/ui stack with React Router for navigation — nothing tied to Lovable's own hosting — and include a vercel.json with an SPA rewrite rule (all routes → index.html) so it deploys on Vercel with zero build errors once synced to GitHub.

Trio Bee Bakes/ Luciano’s lunch line  🧁

 

Mission 

To bake with love and deliver fresh, high-quality treats that bring joy to every home in Umzimkhulu and beyond.  

At Trio Bee Bakes, we’re committed to making delicious, homemade baked goods with the best ingredients, served with warm customer care and reliable delivery.



Vision

To become KwaZulu-Natal’s most loved community bakery, known for creating moments of sweetness and connection, one cake, loaf, and delivery at a time.  

We dream of growing Trio Bee Bakes into a brand that nourishes communities while empowering women in business.



Quick "About Us" blurb for the website*

Founded in August 2021 by *Siviwe Nkete Mwisi*, *Trio B Bakes* is a home-based bakery built on passion, flavor, and service.  

We’re based in *177 Ibisi Township, Umzimkhulu, KwaZulu-Natal*, and we specialize in fresh baking and convenient delivery, so you get that "just out the oven" taste wherever you are.  

Every order is made with care because to us, baking isn’t just business — it’s love.
